## The evidence base (embed-worthy numbers)

- **Fold-chaining, not reviewer thoroughness, drove pass counts.** 29% of real application-code defects (~48 of ~166) existed only because a previous fold created them. Reviews with ≥6 fold-caused findings averaged **10.8 passes**; reviews with ≤1 averaged **3.3**. Two named preventable sub-species: *claims-vs-behavior debris* (~25–30 findings — a correct fix leaving its test name / docstring / log line lying; "none of which any passing test could have caught") and *the un-walked global invariant* (plan-side: a rule folded into one section without walking it through every phase that restates it — self-diagnosed 4× in one plan).
- **The H+M-count stall guardrail never once correctly identified a stall** (0-for-2 in code reviews, both rightly overridden; would have fired 5× indistinguishably on the 12-pass plan). The signal that predicted "stop" in every long loop — *was this finding created by the previous fold?* — was hand-recorded in every pass log and aggregated by nothing.
- **`iterate-plan` has no decline vocabulary.** The runtime-pipeline plan ran 12 passes; the architect lens returned a HIGH 12/12 because every HIGH was structurally mandatory to fold. Passes 9–12 were 4 consecutive, 100% fold-induced, producing acceptance-coverage redistribution and vocabulary hygiene — not design. The v2.3.0 deferral rationale ("plan review hasn't shown the same churn," 2026-08-12) was falsified within 48 hours; the "revisit after Phase 3 evidence" note in the v2.3.0 archive was never revisited. **This plan is that revisit — say so in the Why section.**
- **An unseeded register cost five re-litigations of one settled decision** (doc-bot's Q3 auth-posture position, re-argued across write-path phases 0/1/3 and twice in one later review). Because `iterate-review`'s provenance gate only honors entries predating the diff, late seeding is unrecoverable — seeding at plan time is the highest-leverage cheap action.
- **Disputes/accepted-risks held at ~94%** (1 of ~18 ever re-raised, and that one correctly — falsified and folded). The machinery works where it exists; it exists only in `iterate-review`.
- **Test-only diffs produced zero production bugs in 10 passes** across 4 reviews; one pass re-reviewed a byte-identical diff purely to satisfy the convergence gate.
- **Open-question channel churn:** Q1–Q4 answered identically 12 consecutive times at full lens-input cost. (Precedent for the fix: dropping the PM lens after APPROVE ×3 halved fan-out at zero cost.)
- **This retro cost two mining agents** because all of the above lived in prose. Instrumentation makes the next one a `jq` one-liner.
